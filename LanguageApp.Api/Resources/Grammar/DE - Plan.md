**A1 — Foundations**
1. Personal pronouns + present tense conjugation (regular verbs)
2. W-questions and Yes/No questions
3. Definite/indefinite articles in Nominative (der/die/das, ein/eine)
4. Negation with *kein*
5. Possessive articles (mein, dein...) in Nominative
6. Akkusativ case (articles + basic usage)
7. Modal verbs (können, müssen, wollen, möchten) — present tense
8. Separable verbs (trennbare Verben) — basic
9. Imperative (commands)
10. Perfekt tense — basic, with common verbs (haben/sein + Partizip II)
11. Prepositions with Akkusativ/Dativ (basic: in, auf, mit, für...)

**A2 — Expanding structure**
1. Dativ case fully (articles, pronouns, verbs requiring Dativ)
2. Präteritum of haben/sein/modal verbs
3. Perfekt — expanded (more verbs, irregular Partizip II)
4. Two-way prepositions (Wechselpräpositionen: in, an, auf + Akk/Dat)
5. Reflexive verbs
6. Comparative and superlative (Komparativ/Superlativ)
7. Subordinate clauses with weil, dass, wenn
8. Genitiv case (basic)
9. Adjective declension — introduction
10. Konjunktiv II — basic polite forms (möchte, hätte gern, könnte)

**B1 — Building complexity**
1. Präteritum — all common verbs (used for narration)
2. Plusquamperfekt
3. Passive voice (Passiv Präsens/Präteritum) — introduction
4. Relative clauses (Relativsätze)
5. Adjective declension — full mastery
6. n-Deklination
7. Subordinate clauses expanded: obwohl, während, nachdem, bevor, damit
8. Konjunktiv II — irreal conditions, wishes (present + past)
9. Indirect speech — basic (introduction to Konjunktiv I)
10. Infinitive with zu (um...zu, ohne...zu, statt...zu)
11. Verbs/adjectives/nouns with fixed prepositions

**B2 — Refinement & nuance**
1. Passiv — full system: with modal verbs, Zustandspassiv, Passiversatzformen (sich lassen + Inf., sein + zu + Inf., -bar adjectives)
2. Konjunktiv II — irreal comparisons (als ob), advanced hypotheticals
3. Konjunktiv I — indirect speech, fully (for reported speech, news, quoting)
4. Modal verbs — subjective use (assumptions: er soll/muss/dürfte... sein)
5. Participles as adjectives + Partizipialkonstruktionen (Partizip I/II)
6. Nominalization / nominal style (aufgrund, wegen, trotz + Genitiv)
7. Advanced connectors: trotzdem, dennoch, wohingegen, zudem, vielmehr
8. Nomen-Verb-Verbindungen (einen Antrag stellen, in Frage stellen, in Erwägung ziehen)
9. Complex sentence structure — nested subordinate clauses, word order in Satzgefüge
10. Extended relative clauses with prepositions (das Auto, mit dem...)
